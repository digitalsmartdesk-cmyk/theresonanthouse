How We Work

Four things you can rely on.

These are not aspirations. They are how we operate, from the first conversation onwards.

01

You approve everything before it goes out.

No press release, pitch or statement leaves without your sign-off. Your voice stays yours.

02

We pitch with a specific angle, never a blast.

Every approach is addressed to a person with a reason. Journalists remember the difference.

03

We make no claim we cannot source.

If the evidence does not support the message, we say so and find a better one.

04

We report honestly.

You will hear what is working and what is not, on a regular basis, in plain language.
